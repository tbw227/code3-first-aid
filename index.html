Our Mission

Your Full-Service Safety Partner

Code 3 First Aid is your full-service safety partner for industrial and commercial workplaces across the Midwest. Our primary focus is keeping your facility stocked, compliant, and inspection-ready with first aid supplies and fire extinguishers — supported by reliable monthly or quarterly service visits.

Beyond restocking, we provide hands-on fire extinguisher training, CPR certification, and PPE education for small teams and large operations alike. Our inventory system maintains an accurate supply flow so you never have to track it yourself.
