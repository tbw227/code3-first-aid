Bulk First Aid Supplies FAQs

Can I buy first aid supplies in bulk for a workplace?

Yes. Our collection is specifically designed for businesses and organizations that need to maintain consistent supply levels across multiple locations or high-traffic areas.

How do I know which supplies I need to restock?

We recommend a monthly physical inventory. Our digital tracking sheets can help you identify high-turnover items like bandages and prep pads that require frequent replenishment.

Are these wholesale prices or retail?

These are retail-priced bulk quantities. For very large quarterly orders or institutional procurement contracts, please contact our sales team directly for specialized pricing.

What is the difference between bulk and a kit?

A kit is a pre-assembled emergency solution. Bulk supplies are individual items sold in larger quantities for customizing or restocking existing units without buying entire new shells.
