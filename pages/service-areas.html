Workplace Safety Supplies & Training Service Areas

Code 3 First Aid delivers first aid supplies, fire extinguishers, PPE, and on-site safety training across Missouri, Nebraska, Kansas, and Oklahoma.

Regional Coverage

Code 3 First Aid is a mobile safety equipment supplier and training company — we come to your workplace rather than requiring visits to a retail storefront. Our service-area model covers 15 cities across four states, with on-site delivery and training available within 75 miles of each hub city.

Whether you need ANSI-compliant first aid kit restocking, fire extinguisher inspection and replacement, PPE supply programs, or OSHA-aligned CPR and fire training, we serve employers from northwest Missouri and eastern Nebraska through south-central Kansas and the Oklahoma City and Tulsa metros.
